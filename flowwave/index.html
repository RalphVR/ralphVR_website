How it plays

Remember flying your hand out of a car window?

Flow Wave turns that feeling into a game: a hands-only mixed reality runner where a portal opens in your room onto a vaporwave sunset. No controllers, no buttons, just your hand and the wind.

  1. 01

    Pinch

    A paper plane hovers in front of you. Pinch its keel to pick it up.

  2. 02

    Throw

    Toss it into the portal that tears open in your room.

  3. 03

    Fly

    Roll your hand like a wing to slide. Lift your fingertips to climb.

The world comes to you

Slip through the neon.

Neon blocks, drifting pillars and laser grids pour out of the portal towards you. Grab coins, fly through energy rings to keep gliding, and keep going.

Find your flow

Everything moves to the music.

The blocks, the rings, the grid, even the portal's border pulse on the beat. Fly through gold flow gates at distance milestones to raise your coin multiplier, and your speed. Crash, and the music picks up where you left off.

Risk it

x3. x5. x10.

Multiplier gems pay out more coins, if you can handle the extra speed. Crumple against the neon, and the multiplier is gone.

Build your fleet

Six planes. One is gold.

Spend coins between flights on eight stats for every plane, then unlock the next. The last one, the Golden plane, never runs out of glide.
