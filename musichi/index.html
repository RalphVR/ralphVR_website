Overture con moto

Play a song from your phone, a speaker, anything that makes sound.

Movement I legato

Trace the light.

One movement at a time appears in front of you. Lines of energy coil around you: follow one with your hand from its start to its end, and your hand gathers more and more particles as it goes. At the end, everything bursts.

Follow the line.

Movement II allegretto

Swipe the arcs.

One to three at once, each in a single smooth movement: narrow where it starts, wide where it ends. Each one rings as it goes.

Swipe each arc.

Movement III staccato

Push the orbs.

Glowing orbs in rings of light. Push one away with an open hand and it pops, with a rush of air. Your hands pulse with the beat, and so do the lines of light.

Push an orb.

Interlude tacet

Then, for a while, nothing to do.

Just the music, and the light.

Movement IV lontano

Your room, revealed.

Particles thrown out by your movements glow on your walls where they land. Stay in the flow with the music and, more and more often, the particles map a patch of your room, and the cosmos opens through it: galaxies, nebulae and stars, far beyond your walls.

The wall opens.
