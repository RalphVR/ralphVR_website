Privacy policy

Musichi is a mixed reality music experience for Meta Quest made by VRTogether (“we”). This policy explains what information Musichi uses and what happens to it.

The short version

Musichi uses your headset’s microphone, your tracked hands and your headset’s understanding of your room to turn the music you play into light around you. All of that is processed on your headset, as you play, and then let go. Musichi does not record audio, does not save it, and does not send anything about you, your voice, your hands or your room to us or to anyone else. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What Musichi uses, and why

The microphone. Musichi listens to the music playing in your room so it can respond to it: its loudness, its beats, its notes and its brightness. The sound is analysed on your headset a fraction of a second at a time. The audio is not recorded, stored or transmitted, and the measurements made from it are used only to draw the experience and are not kept. The microphone will also pick up anything else in the room, including voices; that sound is treated the same way and is not kept either. Your headset asks for your permission before Musichi can use the microphone, and you can withdraw it at any time in the headset’s settings (Musichi then has nothing to respond to).

Hand tracking. Musichi follows your hands, as the headset reports them, so you can trace, swipe and push the light. Hand positions are used on your headset, as you play, and are not stored or sent.

Your room (spatial data). With your permission, the headset gives Musichi a description of your room’s surfaces (walls, floor, ceiling, furniture) and how far away things are, so the light can glow on your walls and stay hidden behind real objects. This is used on your headset while Musichi runs and is not stored by Musichi or sent anywhere.

Your head’s position. Musichi places each movement in front of where you are looking. This is used on your headset as you play and is not stored or sent.

What Musichi does not do

  • It does not record audio, video or images, and it does not take screenshots.
  • It does not go online. It works fully offline and sends no information over the internet.
  • It does not ask for or use your name, email, contacts, location, friends list or any account information.
  • It has no advertising, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Deleting your information

Musichi keeps no information about you: it saves nothing on your headset beyond its own files and settings, and nothing reaches us, so there is nothing for us to delete. Uninstalling Musichi from your headset removes everything it installed. To stop it using the microphone or your room’s spatial data, turn those permissions off for Musichi in the headset’s settings (Settings > Privacy & Safety > App permissions). If you believe we hold any information about you, email us at contact email and we will answer, and delete it, within 30 days.

Meta

Musichi is distributed through the Meta Horizon Store. When you start it, your headset confirms with Meta that your account owns it (Meta’s entitlement check); VRTogether receives no information about you from it. Meta’s own handling of your information is described in Meta’s privacy policy.

Children

Musichi is not directed at children under the minimum age Meta sets for using Meta Quest, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

This website

This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and it stores nothing in your browser. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its headline font comes from Google Fonts; like any web host, both receive your IP address when you load a page (see GitHub’s and Google’s privacy policies).

The page can listen too, but only if you ask it to. If you press Listen, your browser asks you for the microphone. If you allow it, the sound is analysed inside your browser, a fraction of a second at a time, to light the page; it is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere, including to us. Press Listen again, or close the page, and the microphone is released. Play a song plays music the page makes itself, in your browser; nothing is downloaded or sent.

Changes

If Musichi changes in a way that affects your information, we will update this page and the date below.
