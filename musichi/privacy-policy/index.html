Musichi Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how RalphVR Inc., doing business as VRTogether (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, maintains, discloses, and protects information collected from players (each, a “Player,” or “you”) of Musichi, as well as visitors to the Musichi website. This Policy applies regardless of the platform on which you obtained or are running Musichi (including, for example, the Meta Horizon Store). By using Musichi, you signify your acceptance of this Policy.

Data Controller

RalphVR Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as VRTogether, is the data controller responsible for the personal information collected in connection with Musichi. You can reach us with privacy-related questions at info@vrtogether.us.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide to us directly, and information provided to us by the distribution platform on which you are running Musichi. Musichi’s features also use some information on your device while you play, which never leaves it.

Information You Provide

We collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily submit to us, such as your name and email address, when you contact us by email.

Data Collected via Distribution Platforms

When you start Musichi, the Meta Horizon Platform confirms that your account owns Musichi (an entitlement check). We do not receive your platform user identifier, display name, or any authentication token from this check, and we never receive your platform account password or your platform payment information.

Information Used by Musichi’s Features

Musichi uses the following on your headset, as you play. None of it is recorded, stored, or transmitted to us or to anyone else.

  • Microphone: Musichi listens to the music playing in your room (its loudness, beats, and notes) so the light can respond to it. The audio is analyzed on your headset a fraction of a second at a time and is not recorded, stored, or transmitted. Your headset asks for your permission first, and you can withdraw it at any time in the headset’s settings.
  • Hand tracking, head position, and your room (spatial data): used so you can trace, swipe, and push the light, so each movement appears in front of you, and so the light can glow on your walls.
  • The Musichi website’s Listen button: if you press it, your browser asks for the microphone. The sound is analyzed in your browser and is never recorded or sent anywhere.

How We Use Collected Information

We collect and use Player information for the following purposes:

  • To run Musichi’s features. The microphone, hand, head, and room data described above are used on your device only, to make Musichi respond to your music and your movement.
  • To improve customer service. Information you provide helps us respond to support requests more efficiently.
  • To respond to inquiries. We may use your email address to respond to your direct questions or requests.

We may also disclose collected information, including personally identifiable information, in any of the following circumstances: (1) where the disclosure is necessary or useful to our provision of Musichi or related services; (2) where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, interests, or property; (3) where we are acting in good faith to protect your safety or the safety of others, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a verified government request; (4) where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law (such as compliance with a subpoena, search warrant, court order, or similar legal or administrative process); or (5) in connection with a sale, merger, acquisition, or other transfer of all or substantially all of the Company’s assets, in which case your information will likely be among the assets transferred.

How We Share Information with Third Parties

We share data only with:

  1. Infrastructure providers that we use to host the Musichi website. Currently this includes GitHub (GitHub Pages) and Google Fonts, which, like any web host, receive your IP address when you load a page.
  2. Distribution platforms that you have chosen to use to access Musichi (such as the Meta Horizon Platform), strictly to confirm that your account owns Musichi.
  3. Parties legally entitled to receive the information under the legal-disclosure conditions described above.

We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as is necessary to provide you with Musichi, satisfy our legitimate business interests, or meet legal obligations:

  • Microphone audio, hand, head, and room data: not retained; used on your device as you play.
  • Customer-support correspondence: retained for up to two (2) years from the last interaction so we can reference prior issues if you contact us again.

Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time, regardless of where you reside. Musichi stores no personal information on our systems; the only personal information we may hold is correspondence you have sent us. To submit a deletion request, email us at info@vrtogether.us with the subject line “Data Deletion Request.”

Upon receiving a verified request, we will delete the personal information associated with you within thirty (30) days, except where we are required by law to retain specific records. Uninstalling Musichi from your headset removes everything it installed there. If you play on the Meta Horizon Platform, you may also initiate a deletion request by uninstalling Musichi and submitting a request through Meta’s account tools; Meta will forward the request to us and we will process it as described above.

How We Protect Your Information

We use industry-standard practices to safeguard your information, including:

  • The Musichi website is served encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to user data is restricted to personnel with a business need.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users without undue delay and in accordance with applicable law, including, where required, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Notifications will describe the nature of the breach, the categories of data involved, the likely consequences, and the steps you can take to protect yourself.

Children’s Privacy

Musichi is not designed to solicit or collect personally identifiable data from individuals under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 for Players in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, where the higher threshold applies under GDPR Article 8). In accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable equivalents, we do not knowingly collect or store any personal information about children under the applicable age.

If we discover we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it within a reasonable time. Parents or guardians who believe we may have collected information from a child under the applicable age may contact us at info@vrtogether.us. Upon reasonable verification, we will delete the child’s information within a reasonable time.

Your Rights

Rights for Players in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are a resident of the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Right of access: to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”): to request that we delete your personal information.
  • Right to restriction of processing: to ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Right to data portability: to receive your information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: with the data protection authority in your country of residence.

The legal bases on which we rely for processing your data are: (a) performance of a contract (operating Musichi and providing the services you request); (b) legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention); (c) consent (microphone and spatial data, which you allow on your headset or in your browser); and (d) legal obligation (responding to lawful requests).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@vrtogether.us. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally one month under GDPR).

Rights for California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right to know the categories of personal information we collect, the sources, the purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties we share it with.
  • Right to access the specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.
  • Right to delete personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing of your personal information. As stated above, VRTogether does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

The categories of personal information we collect are identifiers: your name and email address if you contact us, and your IP address, which our website hosts receive when you load a page.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@vrtogether.us. We will verify your identity before responding and will respond within the 45-day window required by the CCPA.

California “Shine the Light”

Under California’s “Shine the Light” law, California residents who provide personal information in obtaining products or services for personal, family, or household use are entitled to request and obtain from us, once per calendar year, information about the customer information we shared, if any, with other businesses for their own direct-marketing uses during the immediately preceding calendar year. To request this information, send an email to info@vrtogether.us with “Request for California Privacy Information” in the subject line.

Do Not Track

We do not track Players’ activity over time across third-party websites or online services, and we do not build cross-site behavioral profiles. Because Musichi does not engage in cross-site tracking, it does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.

All Other Players

If you reside outside the regions listed above, you may still contact us at info@vrtogether.us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and we will respond as required by applicable law.

By using Musichi, you understand that your information may be transferred to, stored in, and processed in the United States and other countries where our infrastructure providers operate. Where required by law, we use appropriate safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for international transfers of personal data from the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland to the United States.

Updating Your Information

If you believe we hold information about you that needs to be updated or corrected, please contact us at info@vrtogether.us.

Where required by law, we will provide more prominent notice. Your continued use of Musichi following the posting of changes constitutes your acceptance of those changes.
