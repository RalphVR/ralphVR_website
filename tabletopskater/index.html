How it plays

Your fingers are the feet.

Plant your index and middle fingers on the table, so there's a real surface to push against. Roll your back hand and raise your front to ollie, just like a real ollie. The controls grow from there.

  1. Push speed

    Sweep your back fingers toward the tail.

  2. Turn steer

    Front fingers toward an edge to turn. Both hands to carve.

  3. Ollie 100

    Roll your back hand onto its side, then lift your front fingers.

  4. Pop shove-it 250

    As the front fingers lift, slide your back fingers across the tail.

  5. Kickflip 400

    Ollie, hold your front fingers up a beat, then press them down.

  6. And then

    Pinch the board in the air to grab. Spin your hands to spin. Land on a rail to grind. Every trick is designed to feel great to land.
