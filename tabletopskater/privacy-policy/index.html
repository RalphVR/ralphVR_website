Tabletop Skater Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Scope

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) describes how RalphVR Inc., doing business as VRTogether (“Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), collects, uses, maintains, discloses, and protects information collected from players (each, a “Player,” or “you”) of Tabletop Skater, as well as visitors to the Tabletop Skater website. This Policy applies regardless of the platform on which you obtained or are running Tabletop Skater (including, for example, the Meta Horizon Store). By using Tabletop Skater, you signify your acceptance of this Policy.

Data Controller

RalphVR Inc., a Delaware corporation doing business as VRTogether, is the data controller responsible for the personal information collected in connection with Tabletop Skater. You can reach us with privacy-related questions at info@vrtogether.us.

Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways: information you provide to us directly, and information provided through the distribution platform on which you are running Tabletop Skater. Tabletop Skater’s features also use some information on your device while you play, which never leaves it. We operate no servers of our own for Tabletop Skater: its online features run on the Meta Horizon Platform.

Information You Provide

We collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily submit to us, such as your name and email address, when you contact us by email.

Data Collected via Distribution Platforms

When you start Tabletop Skater, the Meta Horizon Platform confirms that your account owns it (an entitlement check). For its leaderboards and friend challenges, Tabletop Skater also uses the following through the Meta Horizon Platform, with the permissions Meta grants it:

  • Your platform user identifier and display name, so your scores can be listed under your name.
  • Your scores and run recordings. When you finish a Score Run, its score is posted to that park’s leaderboard together with a small recording of the run: where the skater was, how high and which way he faced, several times a second, and the name and time of each trick. The recording lets another player’s game replay your run as a ghost. It contains no images, no sound, and nothing about your hands, your table or your room.
  • Your list of friends on the platform who also play Tabletop Skater (their identifiers and display names), so you can see their scores and choose one to challenge.
  • Challenges and invitations. When you challenge a friend, your run is posted for them and the platform sends them an invitation. Their answering run is posted back to you the same way.

Leaderboard entries, challenges and invitations are stored by Meta on the Meta Horizon Platform and are subject to Meta’s own privacy policy. Your display name and scores are visible to other players on the leaderboards, and a challenge is visible to the friend you sent it to. We can see leaderboard entries (display names, scores and run recordings) through Meta’s developer tools. We never receive your platform account password or your platform payment information. If you play offline, none of this is sent; leaderboards and challenges are then unavailable.

Information Used by Tabletop Skater’s Features

Tabletop Skater uses the following on your headset, as you play. None of it is transmitted to us or to anyone else.

  • Hand tracking: the positions of your hands and fingers, so your fingers can control the skater and press the buttons on your table.
  • Your table and room (spatial data): the game uses the headset’s understanding of your surroundings to find your tabletop and place the park on it. So that it can find the same table next time, a spatial anchor and the table’s size are saved on your headset. Your headset asks for your permission first, and you can withdraw it at any time in the headset’s settings. The game does not capture or store camera images.
  • Your progress and settings: the stars and stickers you have earned, your stance, your chosen park and your options are saved on your headset.
  • A session log: the game keeps a short technical log of its own events (for example its set-up steps, the tricks it detected and where your fingertips were relative to the board) for its last ten sessions, on your headset, to help diagnose problems. It is not transmitted.

The Tabletop Skater Website

The website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. Its small skating game runs in your browser and saves nothing. The demo video is hosted by YouTube: nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press Play, after which YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and handles the video under its own privacy policy.

How We Use Collected Information

We collect and use Player information for the following purposes:

  • To run Tabletop Skater’s features. The hand, table and room data described above are used on your device only. Your display name, scores, run recordings and friends list are used to show leaderboards, to replay runs, and to send and answer challenges.
  • To keep leaderboards fair. We may review and remove leaderboard entries that appear to be the result of cheating or errors.
  • To improve customer service. Information you provide helps us respond to support requests more efficiently.
  • To respond to inquiries. We may use your email address to respond to your direct questions or requests.

We may also disclose collected information, including personally identifiable information, in any of the following circumstances: (1) where the disclosure is necessary or useful to our provision of Tabletop Skater or related services; (2) where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, interests, or property; (3) where we are acting in good faith to protect your safety or the safety of others, to investigate fraud, or to respond to a verified government request; (4) where we believe in good faith that disclosure is required by law (such as compliance with a subpoena, search warrant, court order, or similar legal or administrative process); or (5) in connection with a sale, merger, acquisition, or other transfer of all or substantially all of the Company’s assets, in which case your information will likely be among the assets transferred.

How We Share Information with Third Parties

We share data only with:

  1. Distribution platforms that you have chosen to use to access Tabletop Skater (the Meta Horizon Platform), to confirm that your account owns Tabletop Skater and to provide its leaderboards, challenges and invitations as described above.
  2. Other players, who can see your display name, scores and replayed runs on the leaderboards, and the friends you challenge.
  3. Infrastructure providers that we use to host the Tabletop Skater website. Currently this is GitHub (GitHub Pages), which, like any web host, receives your IP address when you load a page, and YouTube if you play the demo video.
  4. Parties legally entitled to receive the information under the legal-disclosure conditions described above.

We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as is necessary to provide you with Tabletop Skater, satisfy our legitimate business interests, or meet legal obligations:

  • Hand, table and room data: not retained by us. The saved table, your progress and the session log stay on your headset until you uninstall the game.
  • Leaderboard entries and challenges: kept on the Meta Horizon Platform for as long as the leaderboards exist, or until they are removed at your request.
  • Customer-support correspondence: retained for up to two (2) years from the last interaction so we can reference prior issues if you contact us again.

Data Deletion Requests

You may request deletion of the personal information we hold about you at any time, regardless of where you reside. To have your leaderboard entries and challenges removed, or to delete correspondence you have sent us, email us at info@vrtogether.us with the subject line “Data Deletion Request” and the display name you play under.

Upon receiving a verified request, we will delete the personal information associated with you within thirty (30) days, except where we are required by law to retain specific records. Uninstalling Tabletop Skater from your headset removes everything it saved there. If you play on the Meta Horizon Platform, you may also initiate a deletion request through Meta’s account tools; Meta will forward the request to us and we will process it as described above.

How We Protect Your Information

We use industry-standard practices to safeguard your information, including:

  • Tabletop Skater talks only to the Meta Horizon Platform, over encrypted connections that the platform provides.
  • The Tabletop Skater website is served encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Access to user data is restricted to personnel with a business need.

No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure, however, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of your information.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify affected users without undue delay and in accordance with applicable law, including, where required, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. Notifications will describe the nature of the breach, the categories of data involved, the likely consequences, and the steps you can take to protect yourself.

Children’s Privacy

Tabletop Skater is not designed to solicit or collect personally identifiable data from individuals under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 for Players in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, where the higher threshold applies under GDPR Article 8). In accordance with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and applicable equivalents, we do not knowingly collect or store any personal information about children under the applicable age.

If we discover we have inadvertently collected such information, we will delete it within a reasonable time. Parents or guardians who believe we may have collected information from a child under the applicable age may contact us at info@vrtogether.us. Upon reasonable verification, we will delete the child’s information within a reasonable time.

Your Rights

Rights for Players in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are a resident of the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have the following rights with respect to your personal information:

  • Right of access: to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification: to ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to erasure (“right to be forgotten”): to request that we delete your personal information.
  • Right to restriction of processing: to ask us to limit how we use your information.
  • Right to data portability: to receive your information in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to object: to object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Right to withdraw consent: where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: with the data protection authority in your country of residence.

The legal bases on which we rely for processing your data are: (a) performance of a contract (operating Tabletop Skater and providing the features you use, including leaderboards and challenges); (b) legitimate interests (security, fraud prevention, fair leaderboards); (c) consent (hand tracking and spatial data, which you allow on your headset); and (d) legal obligation (responding to lawful requests).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@vrtogether.us. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (generally one month under GDPR).

Rights for California Residents (CCPA / CPRA)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights:

  • Right to know the categories of personal information we collect, the sources, the purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties we share it with.
  • Right to access the specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you in the past 12 months.
  • Right to delete personal information we have collected about you, subject to certain exceptions.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing of your personal information. As stated above, VRTogether does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Right to non-discrimination for exercising any of these rights.

The categories of personal information we collect are identifiers: your platform user identifier and display name with your leaderboard entries, your name and email address if you contact us, and your IP address, which our website host receives when you load a page.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@vrtogether.us. We will verify your identity before responding and will respond within the 45-day window required by the CCPA.

California “Shine the Light”

Under California’s “Shine the Light” law, California residents who provide personal information in obtaining products or services for personal, family, or household use are entitled to request and obtain from us, once per calendar year, information about the customer information we shared, if any, with other businesses for their own direct-marketing uses during the immediately preceding calendar year. To request this information, send an email to info@vrtogether.us with “Request for California Privacy Information” in the subject line.

We do not track Players’ activity over time across third-party websites or online services, and we do not build cross-site behavioral profiles. Because Tabletop Skater does not engage in cross-site tracking, it does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals.
